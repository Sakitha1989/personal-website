Privacy policy for "mini"

"mini" is a personal assistant used only by its owner, Sakitha Ariyarathne. It is not offered to anyone else.

What it accesses

It signs in to its own Google account and uses the Google Calendar API to read that account's calendars (and any calendar the owner has shared with it) and to add events to its own calendar.

How the data is used and stored

Calendar data is used only to answer the owner's questions and keep the owner's schedule. It is processed on the owner's own computer. It is not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising. Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Access can be withdrawn at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Contact

Sakitha Ariyarathne, through the contact details on the home page.
